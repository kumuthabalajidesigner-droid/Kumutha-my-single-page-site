About

Designing for complexity, across industries

I'm a Senior Product Designer who has spent more than a decade turning complex enterprise systems into products people understand, trust and want to use.

My career started in visual storytelling, with infographics for The Hindu and motion graphics for television. It has grown into leading end-to-end product design for global enterprises at Cognizant, Infosys and now Cloutics. Along the way I've kept one habit: I start with the people using the system and work back to the technology.

What drives me

Curiosity about users, businesses, new technology and the people I build with. I love the moment a tangled workflow becomes obvious, when a claims analyst, a dispatcher or a benefits enrollee can finish their task without having to think about the software. Lately that curiosity is focused on AI: designing conversational experiences that are transparent, grounded, and honest about what they don't know.

10+ years of experience

I've done every part of the craft: user research and moderated usability testing, information architecture, journey maps and service blueprints, wireframes, high-fidelity UI, interactive prototypes, design systems and developer handoff. The work spans web, tablet and mobile, and has moved from pixel-level visual design to product strategy and AI interaction models.

Leading projects

I lead design from discovery through delivery: running stakeholder workshops, aligning product, engineering and business goals, setting priorities and reducing delivery risk. At Infosys I ran a UX roadshow that positioned UX as a strategic capability, supported pre-sales with proof-of-concept designs, and mentored designers. At Cloutics I lead the Conversational and Generative AI experience for an ICHRA benefits platform.

Enterprise applications

My home ground is complex, data-heavy B2B software: role-based dashboards for 7+ user types, metric-configuration and approval workflows, bulk automation, multi-tenant healthcare platforms, Power BI and Tableau reporting, and design systems that cut design-to-development handoff time by 50%. I design within real constraints like compliance, legacy systems and accessibility (WCAG), and I measure success by adoption and efficiency.
